Roadmap, probably

What kind of roadmap can a meme coin even have?

CHEPE was launched for fun. To make people laugh, create memes, build something weird together, and maybe make some money along the way.

But sometimes the best things start as jokes and slowly become something bigger. The more time we spend building CHEPE, the more convinced we become that this strange little creature has the potential to become one of TON's cult meme characters.

We are not abandoning this project.

At the same time, we are not going to scream about it 24/7, spam every chat, or burn ourselves out trying to force growth.

TON moves at its own pace. Telegram moves at its own pace. Communities grow at their own pace. We'll build CHEPE the same way: slowly, consistently, and for the long term.

What we're actively working on

  • Posting the best CHEPE memes created by the community.
  • Growing and maintaining our social media accounts.
  • Creating short-form videos for TikTok, Reels, and Shorts.
  • Running giveaways, contests, and Telegram Gifts.
  • Attracting creative, active, and talented TON users.
  • Expanding the CHEPE website and ecosystem.
  • Buying advertising whenever our budget allows it.
  • Supporting community-driven content and initiatives.

Most importantly: CHEPERATOR

What started as a joke bot is slowly becoming the heart of the CHEPE ecosystem. Our long-term goal is to turn CHEPERATOR into a genuinely useful tool for Telegram and TON users, while keeping the fun, chaos, and meme spirit intact.

This roadmap will evolve as we evolve. No deadlines. No promises. Just building.
